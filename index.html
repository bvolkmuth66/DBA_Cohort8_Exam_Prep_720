One estimator, three names

Worth noticing as you work through these. The same quantity — Cov(x,y) / Var(y) — appears as beta in the market model, as the minimum-variance hedge ratio in the derivatives module, and as the market-model slope that generates expected returns in the event study. Three applications, one regression coefficient. Naming that connection in an essay costs a sentence and shows the quantitative material is understood as one structure rather than four separate procedures.
