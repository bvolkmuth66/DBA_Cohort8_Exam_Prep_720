DBA 720 Comprehensive Exam Prep Lab

Excel practice for the computational half of the examination.

What the instructor said. Most questions will be essay-based, with the syllabus reading list as the primary preparation resource. He will not ask in Excel for tasks completed in Python. Excel was used in class for derivatives, and students “may want to” review that material and the DerivaGem spreadsheet. And: “Please also review how to perform regression analysis in Excel, along with basic statistical functions such as mean, variance, covariance, correlation, etc that you learned in your statistics course.”
How this lab is weighted. Those last two instructions are not equally strong. Derivatives are something you “may want to” review; the statistics carry “please also review” and are framed as refreshing what you already learned. The expected exam task is a supplied dataset and a narrative, with descriptive statistics, correlation and regression done in Excel and interpreted — not formulas reconstructed from memory. The modules are ordered to match.

One estimator, three names

Worth noticing as you work through these. The same quantity — Cov(x,y) / Var(y) — appears as beta in the market model, as the minimum-variance hedge ratio in the derivatives module, and as the market-model slope that generates expected returns in the event study. Three applications, one regression coefficient. Naming that connection in an essay costs a sentence and shows the quantitative material is understood as one structure rather than several separate procedures.
