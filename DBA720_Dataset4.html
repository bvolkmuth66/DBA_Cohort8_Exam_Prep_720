Interpretation Questions

1. Why is trust important in cryptocurrency wallet adoption?


Trust plays a critical role because users must feel confident that their assets, transactions, and data are secure. Even highly usable systems may fail to retain users if trust is lacking.

2. Which variable had the strongest influence on Behavioral Intention?


The strongest predictor is the variable with the largest statistically significant regression coefficient. This must be determined from the regression output rather than the correlation matrix.

3. Would improving usability alone guarantee continued wallet usage?


No. Usability may increase trust and improve user experience, but trust ultimately plays a critical role in determining continued usage intentions.

4. What recommendations would you make to wallet developers?


Developers should improve usability, simplify navigation, strengthen reliability, provide better security communication, and focus on building user trust through transparency and consistent performance.
