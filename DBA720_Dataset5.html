Interpretation Questions

1. Based on your regression results, which factor had the greatest impact on platform adoption?


The strongest predictor is the independent variable with the largest statistically significant regression coefficient. This determination should be based on the regression output rather than the correlation matrix alone.

2. Were all hypotheses supported?


Hypothesis support should be evaluated using both coefficient direction and statistical significance. A positive coefficient alone does not automatically indicate support if the predictor is not significant.

3. What are the primary managerial implications?


Management should prioritize investment in the factors that demonstrate the greatest influence on platform adoption. Resources should be allocated to improvements that have both practical and statistical significance.

4. What recommendations would you make to regulators?


Regulators should emphasize transparency, security, investor protection, compliance, and auditability. These factors help improve trust and encourage safe adoption of blockchain-based financial systems.

5. Explain how blockchain may improve financial markets.


Blockchain can improve market transparency, reduce transaction costs, increase settlement speed, improve security, and reduce reliance on intermediaries. These benefits may contribute to greater adoption and more efficient financial systems.
