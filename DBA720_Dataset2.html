Interpretation Questions

1. Which factor appears to have the greatest impact on customer satisfaction?

The factor with the greatest impact on customer satisfaction is the variable with the largest significant regression coefficient. While correlation helps identify relationships, regression analysis determines each variable's unique contribution after controlling for other predictors.

2. Were all hypotheses supported?

Hypothesis support should be determined using the regression results. A hypothesis is generally supported when the coefficient is in the expected direction and the predictor is statistically significant.

3. What recommendations would you make to management?

Management should prioritize the factors identified as significant predictors in the regression model. Resources should be focused on improvements that are most likely to increase customer satisfaction.

4. If management could improve only one area, which area should they prioritize and why?

If only one area can be improved, management should focus on the strongest statistically significant predictor identified by the regression analysis. Doing so will likely produce the greatest improvement in customer satisfaction.
